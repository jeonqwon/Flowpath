# OpenReclaim — User Guide

## Getting Started

When you first open OpenReclaim, you'll see an empty timeline and four tabs at the bottom: **Tasks**, **Planner**, **Reminders**, and **Settings**. Start by setting up your daily structure, then add tasks.

---

## 1. Set Up Time Periods (Settings tab)

Time periods divide your day into labeled blocks, helping the scheduler find the right slot for each task.

1. Tap the **Settings** tab
2. Tap **Add Period** to define a new block:
   - **Label** — e.g. "Morning Deep Work", "Lunch", "Evening"
   - **Start / End time** — when this period runs
   - **Type** — **Productive** (work-eligible) or **Life** (personal, tasks won't be scheduled here)
3. Periods appear as colored sections on the timeline

*You can add, edit, or remove periods at any time.*

---

## 2. Create a Task

1. Tap the **+** button (bottom-right on Tasks tab)
2. Fill in the task form:
   - **Title** — required
   - **Description** — optional notes
   - **Due date & time** — deadline
   - **Estimated duration** — how long the task takes (15 min – 8 hours)
   - **Priority** — Low / Medium / High / Urgent
   - **Preferred time of day** — Anytime, Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Night
   - **Preferred period** — tie the task to a specific time period
   - **Repeat** — None, Daily, or Weekly (select days)
   - **Add reminder** — creates a linked reminder notification
3. Tap **Save Task**

The task appears on the **Tasks** timeline at its scheduled time.

---

## 3. Create a Reminder

1. Tap the **+** button, then switch to **Reminder** tab at the top
2. Enter title, description, due time, and optional repeat
3. Optionally link to an existing task
4. Tap **Save Reminder**

Reminders appear in the **Reminders** tab and can be dismissed, completed, or snoozed.

---

## 4. The Day View (Tasks tab)

The main **Tasks** screen shows your day as a scrollable timeline:

- **Hour labels** on the left edge
- **Colored time periods** as background blocks
- **Task blocks** with title, duration, and priority color
- **Reminders** at their due times
- A live **"now" indicator** showing your current position in the day

### Task Actions

Tap any task block to open its detail view where you can:
- **Complete** — mark it done (shaded block, moves to history)
- **Reschedule** — move to the next available slot or fit urgently
- **Delete** — remove the task
- **Lock / Unlock** — prevent or allow the task from being moved by the scheduler
- **Add follow-up** — create a new task pre-filled with this task's data
- **Link reminder** — attach a reminder to the task

---

## 5. The Planner (Calendar view)

The **Planner** tab shows a month calendar grid:

- **Dots** on days that have tasks scheduled
- Tap a day to see its scheduled tasks and reminders in a day-overview panel
- Use **< >** arrows to navigate months

---

## 6. Reminders Tab

Shows all reminders sorted by time. Each reminder can be:
- **Completed** — marks it done
- **Snoozed** — hides it temporarily
- **Deleted** — removes it permanently

Reminders linked to tasks show the task title as a reference.

---

## 7. Scheduling

The scheduler automatically places tasks into available time slots based on:
- Your **work hours** (9:00–12:00, 13:00–17:00 by default)
- Your **time periods** (tasks prefer their linked productive periods)
- Task **priority** and **deadline urgency**
- **Existing locked blocks** and completed tasks

When you add, complete, or reschedule a task, the schedule rebuilds automatically.

---

## 8. Tips

- **Start small** — add 2-3 time periods first, then a couple of tasks
- **Lock important blocks** — prevents the scheduler from moving them
- **Use repeat** for daily/weekly routines (recurring tasks show as a series)
- **Link reminders to tasks** — reminders stay associated with their parent task
- **Reschedule missed tasks** — tap a missed task and choose "Reschedule" from its detail view
