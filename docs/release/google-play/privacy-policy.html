OpenReclaim Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Overview

OpenReclaim is a local-first task planning app. In the current Android release, your tasks, reminders, schedule blocks, and time periods are stored on your device.

Data We Process

OpenReclaim processes the information you enter into the app, including:

  • task titles and descriptions
  • reminders and reminder descriptions
  • due dates, recurrence settings, and time periods
  • schedule and completion state generated by the local planner

How Data Is Used

  • to create your task schedule
  • to show reminders inside the app
  • to manage recurring tasks and follow-ups
  • to preserve your planner data between app launches

Data Sharing

OpenReclaim does not share your personal data with third parties in the current Android release.

Data Collection

OpenReclaim does not transmit your planner data to a remote server in the current Android release.

Local Storage

Planner data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database.

Notifications and External Services

The current Android release does not request notification permission and does not sync with external calendar accounts.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Children

OpenReclaim is not directed to children under 13.
